# Getting Started with ByggKPI – Construction Industry Insights

Welcome to **ByggKPI**, your Power BI Template App for exploring construction industry trends using official data from Statistics Norway (SSB). Follow these steps to get started:

---

## 1. Install the App
- Go to [Microsoft AppSource](https://appsource.microsoft.com).
- Search for **ByggKPI – Construction Industry Insights**.
- Click **Get it now** and sign in with your Microsoft account.
- Choose the workspace where you want to install the app.

---

## 2. Connect to Data
- When prompted, click **Connect** to link the app to the SSB API.
- No credentials are required—the API is public and read-only.
- Ensure your Power BI service can access `https://data.ssb.no/api`.

---

## 3. Refresh the Dataset
- After installation, open **Settings > Dataset**.
- Schedule automatic refreshes to keep your data up to date.

---

## 4. Explore the Reports
- Navigate through the five thematic reports and the overview page.
- Use slicers and filters to select time periods, dimensions, and variables.
- Hover over charts for detailed values and trends.

---

## 5. Customize and Share
- Apply filters to create tailored views.
- Export visuals for presentations or share dashboards with your team.
